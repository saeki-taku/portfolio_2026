# SP spring / awake night v1

Review-candidate layered artwork at 1024 x 1997, origin `(0, 0)`. Draw the RGBA files in `manifest.json` order. It uses the approved SP day v21 figure, exterior, window and room geometry. The generated SP clear-night study supplied only broad blue/warm light and locations of discrete city lights; the figure, legs and playground were not redrawn.

`01a-city-lights.png` sits above the exterior and below the window/interior. The subtle right-side reading glow is baked into the relit room and figure layers. Hide `07-rin-occluded-underpaint.png` and `08-rin-notebook.png` together for `preview-no-rin.png`; no old sketchbook or contact residue remains. The cup and plant toggle groups from SP day v21 are retained in `manifest.json`, but their static painted edges should not be independently moved or scaled.

All original alpha and coordinates were preserved by a pointwise low-pass lighting transfer. The v21 background-completion and linked underpaint layers were relit and the no-Rin result inspected, not mixed unaltered from daytime. City lights are fixed to this SP exterior; they are not an independently editable or PC-shared master.
